[Publication] Multi-objective design optimization of educational buildings using CatBoost and NSGA-III under future climate scenarios

September 2026

This study develops a multi-objective design optimization framework for educational buildings under future climate scenarios (2050 and 2080). By integrating CMIP6-based climate projections, CatBoost surrogate modeling, and NSGA-III, the study evaluates energy consumption, daylighting, and thermal comfort across three school building typologies. The CatBoost model accelerates simulations by approximately 180 times, while the optimization identifies window-to-wall ratio, building orientation, shading configuration, and wall materials as key design factors, supporting climate-adaptive and energy-efficient building design.

[Publication Details]

• Journal: Energy (2026)
• Authors: Yeqin Shen, Steven Jige Quan, Yingnan Li, Waishan Qiu, Yuankai Wang, Junga Lee
• DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2026.141386

[Publication] Nonlinear relationship between urban form and heat resilience: An empirical study of Seoul

September 2026

This study examines the nonlinear relationship between urban form and urban heat resilience (HR) in Seoul using a land surface temperature-based proxy in 2016. By applying a Spatial Durbin Model (SDM) and gradient boosting decision trees (GBDT), the findings reveal that vegetation and water presence consistently enhance HR, whereas building coverage shows a strong negative association, emphasizing the importance of green/blue infrastructure and urban openness.

[Publication Details]

• Journal: Building and Environment (2026)
• Authors: Jianqi Li, Lili Lin, Danbi Kim, Chaosu Li, Wei Wang, Steven Jige Quan
• DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.115226

[Publication] Mixture of urban form archetypes in Seoul: A signal processing approach

September 2026

This study models urban form as mixtures of latent archetypes using a simplex-constrained non-negative matrix factorization (SimplexNMF) algorithm applied to 2019 GIS indicators in Seoul. The findings recover nine interpretable archetypes and identify nine archetype-dominant and six mixed clusters, offering a scalable framework to diagnose neighborhood character and guide context-sensitive urban regeneration.

[Publication Details]

• Journal: URBAN DESIGN International (2026)
• Authors: Steven Jige Quan, Sihyeon Kim
• DOI: https://doi.org/10.1057/s41289-026-00349-z

[Publication] Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Energy-Efficient Urban Planning

June 2026

Traditional urban planning methods often fail to account for the complex, multi-tiered interdependencies between urban structures, land use, and building design. To address this challenge, this study introduces a Hierarchical Energy-Efficient Urban Planning (HEEUP) framework based on hierarchical reinforcement learning (HRL). By integrating decision-making across three distinct levels, the proposed framework enables cascading agents to collaborate on energy-conscious planning decisions. Evaluated on real-world datasets from Miami and Albuquerque, HEEUP achieved a 7.3% improvement in energy efficiency compared to baseline methods and reduced computational training time by an average of 24.52%, offering a scalable solution for sustainable urban planning.

[Publication Details]

• Journal: ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology (2026)
• Authors: Yanan Xiao, Lu Jiang, Steven Jige Quan, Pengfei Wang, Minghao Yin, Pengyang Wang
• DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3810947
